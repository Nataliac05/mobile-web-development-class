# Preferences

## Photo edits (face swaps, recoloring, captions)

- **Face swap:** Replace the whole face, including the forehead and hairline, and blend it into the original hair. It should look natural, as if the person was in the original photo, not like a face pasted onto someone else's head. Keep the expression and skin tone believable for the scene.
- **Recoloring clothes:** Use a bright, saturated color (for example a vivid true red, not a dull or dark red). Color the whole garment, including the collar, placket and cuffs. Keep the fabric's folds and shading, and don't color skin, ties or cords.
- **Captions:**
  - Use a big, heavy, rounded sans-serif font (Nunito Black/ExtraBold style), white with a soft dark drop shadow and no hard outline.
  - Make the text large, about 1/12 of the image height per line, and wrap it to two lines.
  - Put it in the requested corner with comfortable padding.
  - End the sentence with a period, not an exclamation mark, unless asked.
- **Output:** Save as a full-resolution JPG and send it as a downloadable file.
